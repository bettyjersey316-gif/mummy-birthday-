A letter for my Mummy

Happy Birthday to my Mummy, my heart, my safe place, and one of the biggest blessings of my life. ❤️🥹🎂🌹

Mummy, honestly, I don’t even know where to start because sometimes I feel like no amount of words can truly explain how much you mean to me. Today is your special day, and all I can think about is how blessed I am to have you as my mother. If there is one thing I will never take for granted in this life, it is your love, your support, your prayers, and all the sacrifices you have made for me. ❤️

Mummy, thank you for loving me the way you do. Thank you for always wanting the best for me, for praying for me even when I may not know it, for supporting me, encouraging me, and believing in me. Thank you for the things you do quietly, the sacrifices you make without complaining, and the love you give me without expecting anything in return. I may not always know how to express my gratitude, but please know that I see you, I appreciate you, and I carry your love in my heart every single day. 🥹❤️

Sometimes, I wish I could let you see yourself through my eyes, just so you would understand how special you are to me. You are more than just my mother; you are my biggest supporter, my cheerleader, my comfort, and one of the people whose happiness means the world to me. There is something so special about being your daughter, about knowing that I have a mother whose love and prayers I can always cherish. No matter how old I become, I will always be your girl, your baby, and someone who will forever need and appreciate her Mummy. 🫂❤️

Mummy, I pray that God blesses you in ways that will leave you speechless. May He reward you for every sacrifice, answer every prayer in your heart, and give you all the beautiful things you have wished for. May you enjoy the fruits of your labour, live long in good health, and experience so much happiness that all your worries become distant memories. May God continue to protect you, strengthen you, uplift you, and surround you with the same love you have always shown me.

One of my biggest prayers is that I get to see you enjoy life, smile genuinely, achieve your dreams, and experience beautiful things. I pray that I will have many more years to celebrate you, make you proud, give you reasons to smile, and show you just how much you mean to me. I want to be able to give you beautiful memories, make life easier for you, and love you in all the ways you deserve to be loved. You have done so much for me, Mummy, and I pray that life gives me the opportunity to give back to you in ways that make your heart happy. ❤️🙏🏽

Thank you for being my mother. Thank you for being my Mummy. Thank you for every prayer, every sacrifice, every act of kindness, and every moment you have shown me love. I do not take you for granted, and I pray that God never allows me to lose sight of the blessing I have in you.

Happy birthday, my beautiful Mummy. 🥹❤️🎂🌹 May this new age bring you peace, joy, divine favour, answered prayers, and blessings beyond your imagination. May you never know sorrow that overwhelms you, and may your heart always have reasons to be happy. May God preserve you for me because I still have so much love to give you, so many memories to make with you, and so many more birthdays I want us to celebrate together.

I love you so much, Mummy. More than I say, more than I show, and more than these words could ever explain. If I had the chance to choose a mother, I would still pray for someone like you. Being your daughter is a blessing I will forever be grateful for.

Happy birthday, my Mummy, my forever cheerleader, my answered prayer, and my precious gift from God. May your days be long, your smile never fade, and your happiness never run dry. I love you endlessly, Mum’s girl forever. ❤️🥹🫂🎉

With all my love, always,
Favour ❤️🌹

A prayer from my heart

A Prayer for My Mummy 🙏🏽❤️

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for the precious gift of my Mummy. Thank You for her life, her love, her kindness, and the countless ways she has been a blessing to me. Lord, today I place her in Your loving hands and ask You to surround her with Your presence, protection, and unfailing grace.

Father, please bless her with long life and good health. Renew her strength each day, give her peaceful rest, and protect her wherever she goes. Let no harm come near her, and may Your angels watch over her in all her ways. When she feels tired, be her strength; when she feels worried, be her peace; and when life feels heavy, remind her that she is never alone.

Lord, answer the prayers she has whispered in secret and remember every good thing she has done for others. Reward her sacrifices, bless the work of her hands, and let her enjoy the fruits of her labour. Open doors of favour and opportunities for her. May she never lack the help, provision, love, or support she needs.

Fill her days with genuine happiness, laughter, beautiful surprises, and people who treat her with the same love and care she gives so freely. Replace every worry with reassurance, every sadness with comfort, and every disappointment with renewed hope. Let this new year of her life bring testimonies, answered prayers, divine direction, and blessings beyond what she could imagine.

Father, please preserve her for many more years. Let me have the joy of celebrating many birthdays with her, making beautiful memories, making her proud, and giving back to her in meaningful ways. Give me the wisdom and strength to love, honour, appreciate, and care for her as she deserves.

May Your goodness and mercy follow my Mummy all the days of her life. May her home be filled with peace, her heart with joy, and her future with hope. Let her always know how deeply she is loved by You and by me. May she flourish in every season and live to see many of her heart's desires fulfilled.

In Jesus’ mighty name, Amen. 🙏🏽❤️

Mummy, may God keep you for me and make your years ahead more beautiful than the years behind you. I love you endlessly. ❤️🌹
